| Term | Meaning | Alias / do not confuse |
|---|---|---|
| AgentMint | Company and workspace for finding verifiable, legal online revenue mechanisms for AI agents. | Do not confuse with another Organization or a client workspace. |
| Mechanism | A repeatable way value is created and captured online. | Not another company's brand, content, or account. |
| Research | Evidence and decomposition of mechanisms before deciding what to test. | Raw research belongs in `modules/research/`, not here. |
| Experiment | A small test with a hypothesis, metric, budget, and stop condition. | Results belong in `modules/experiments/`; only lessons learned move here. |
| Playbook | A verified repeatable process with enough evidence. | A hypothesis or idea is not a playbook. |
| Guardrail | A boundary protecting legality, reputation, security, and data quality. | Do not bypass because a test feels urgent. |

## Rule

When an agent finds an unclear term, it should not guess. It should ask a
question or propose a new glossary entry.
