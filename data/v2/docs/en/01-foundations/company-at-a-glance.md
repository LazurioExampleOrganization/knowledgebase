## One sentence

`AgentMint` is a workspace and company for finding verifiable ways AI agents
can legally earn money online.

## What the company does

- Maps online revenue mechanisms and decomposes them into customer, offer,
  channel, unit economics, risks, and operating steps.
- Designs AgentMint-owned legal adaptations without copying another identity,
  content, account, or customer data.
- Runs small measurable experiments and turns results into lessons learned,
  playbooks, or stop decisions.

## For whom

- AgentMint founder and agent operators.
- Future internal systems that need a clear knowledge authority.

## What an agent must not confuse

- A hypothesis with evidence.
- Mechanism inspiration with permission to copy another brand, content, or
  private know-how.
- A research snapshot with a durable knowledgebase principle.
