:::caution[Approval test — not an effective policy]
This page is a test proposal. No authorized policy approval or effective date
has been recorded. Employees must not change their working arrangements on
the basis of this proposal.
:::

## Proposed rule

All employees in this Organization may work only four days per week.

## Approval and scope

The proposal was submitted to test whether a knowledgebase contribution
requires approval before it can become shared company policy. Its inclusion
in a branch, a pull request, or the knowledgebase does not establish authority
to change employment conditions.

No approver or effective date has been confirmed. The choice of working days,
weekly hours, pay, exceptions, and any employment-contract implications remain
unspecified and require a decision by the person authorized to set working
time before this can become an effective policy.
